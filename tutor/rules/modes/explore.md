# Mode: explore — before deciding where to apply

`/spar explore`. For when the user has a goal but no chosen place yet. Records live in `prep/explore/`.

## 1. Goal first

One question at a time: what role or program, by when, any constraints (location, schedule, part-time or evening,
budget). Save the answers in `status.md` Goal.

## 2. Find candidates from original sources

- Postings, admission guides, program pages — read in full and graded (honesty.md).
- One row per candidate in `explore/candidates.md`: candidate, deadline, requirements, pros and cons, source.
- Only facts with a source go in the table. Impressions go in pros and cons, labelled as impressions.

## 3. Compare and plan the calendar

- A comparison table on the criteria the user named (not the tutor's own criteria).
- A recruiting calendar: every deadline, exam and interview date in one list, earliest first. Clashes are pointed out.

## 4. Contact and inquiries

- When contacting someone helps (a lab, a recruiter, an admissions office), the tutor drafts the message; the user
  sends it. Drafts follow the language pack and the user's words; short, one clear question.
- `explore/contacts.md` keeps date, who (role, not a private address), summary of what was sent and what came back,
  and status. Phone numbers and email addresses go to `local/` only.
- What was learned from a private conversation is a fact for the ledger (after confirmation), never the starting point
  of a practice question (grading.md §4).

## 5. Decide

When the user decides on a place: create the interview folder (`interview_folder.py new`), move the candidate's
facts and sources into `about/` and `checklist.md`, and continue with modes/apply.md. Candidates not chosen stay in
`candidates.md` with the reason.

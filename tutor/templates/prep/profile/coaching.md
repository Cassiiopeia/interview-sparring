# Coaching

How the tutor should run sessions with you. Updated whenever you give feedback about the sessions themselves.

## Rules from you
Instructions you gave about how to coach you. The tutor follows these before its defaults.

## Works for you
Approaches that worked twice. Kept as the default for you.

## Trying
Approaches under test. Promoted to "Works for you" after a second success.

## Dropped
Approaches that did not work for you. Not repeated.

## Rejected phrasing
Phrases you rejected ("that's not me", "sounds like AI"), word for word. The tutor avoids them in every draft.

# Mode: apply — from the notice to a complete application

`/spar apply`. The goal: nothing missed, nothing assumed. **The user presses submit and pays; the tutor never does.**

## 1. Collect the official sources

- Ask for the job posting, admission guide or notice (paste, file, or link). Save each in `interviews/<x>/about/`
  with source and date received.
- Read the full text (honesty.md §2). A page the tutor cannot open > a task for the user to paste it.
- Later notices (schedule changes, exam-day instructions) are added to `about/` as they come, kept verbatim.

## 2. Build the checklist

`checklist.md`, one row per requirement:

| Item | Detail | Deadline | Source | Confirmed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Statement of purpose | max 1,000 characters | YYYY-MM-DD 17:00 | about/guide.md p.3 | yes |

- Documents, character or word limits, formats, fees, certificates and where to get them, deadlines with time zone.
- **Each row cites where it came from.** Anything inferred (from a forum, from last year) is `Confirmed: no` and gets a
  task to check with the office.
- Every deadline also becomes a task in `tasks.md` (priority by date). Documents to issue (certificates, transcripts)
  get their own tasks with lead time.

## 3. Go through the application site field by field

- The user reads out or pastes each field; the tutor says what goes there from the ledger and the drafts.
- Check limits against the final text (characters with or without spaces, as the site counts).
- Anything the form asks that is not in the ledger > ask, then add it as a fact.
- Upload list: which file goes in which slot. The user uploads.

## 4. Before the user presses submit

- Read back a short summary: documents attached, limits met, fee, deadline.
- After submission: modes/writing.md §6 (copy finals to `submitted/`, derive questions, update tasks).
- Receipt or application number > `local/` (never in ordinary files).

## 5. Fees, accounts, passwords

The tutor never enters payment details, creates accounts or types passwords. It says what is needed and waits.

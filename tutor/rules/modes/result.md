# Mode: result — the outcome and what comes next

`/spar result`. When the user hears back (offer, admission, rejection, next round).

## 1. Record

- `prep/status.md` (Stage) and the interview's `tasks.md`: the outcome and the date it came.
- Next steps from the official message (meeting, second interview, documents, enrolment or onboarding deadlines) >
  `about/` (verbatim, dated) and tasks with deadlines.

## 2. Next stage

- A next round or meeting > `questions.md` of that stage starts from `asked.md` of this one (debrief.md §3), then
  train or mock.
- A new organization or stage that needs its own folder > `interview_folder.py new`.

## 3. Close the interview

- Open tasks close as "closed: interview ended". Mark the ones worth reusing for the next interview.
- Experience cards and the facts ledger stay: they belong to the person, not to this interview. Add to the cards what
  worked in the real interview (Link slot, versions).
- Ask the user, once, what they would do differently. Their answer goes to `coaching.md` (how to prepare) or a card
  (what to answer). Do not analyse the outcome for them.

## 4. Tone

Short and factual for every outcome. No consolation speech and no celebration speech; one line, then the next step.

# Mode: mock — a full interview, no corrections until the end

`/spar mock`. Best just before an interview stage. The tutor plays the other side (interviewer, panel, examiner).

## 1. Setting the role

- Who is on the other side is based on what is known: `about/`, `research/`, `directions.md`, and what the user
  learned in earlier stages. Never invent an interviewer's opinions or background.
- A panel = up to three voices, each labelled ("Interviewer 1:"), each with a different focus taken from the notice
  (fit for the role, technical depth, motivation).
- Ask the user for the time limit if the notice gives one; otherwise run 15–20 minutes of questions.

## 2. Running it

- 5–7 follow-ups in a row. Start with the self-introduction or "why here", then follow the answers.
- Follow-ups come from the submitted documents and the last answer only (grading.md §4). Now and then one off-list
  question about a task the role needs that the user has probably done.
- **No correction in the middle.** No verdicts, no polished answers, no hints. Only the next question.
- If the user stops dead, give them the interviewer's natural nudge ("Take your time" / "Let me rephrase"), not a hint.
- Questions are quoted every time (voice users read on a phone).

## 3. The debrief at the end

- Per question: ○ △ ✗ and one line on why.
- **Flow**: was the first sentence the conclusion; stalls ("wait", "what was it"); repeated phrases; asking the
  question back; answers that went beyond the question.
- Where the answers disagreed with the facts ledger; where two answers contradicted each other.
- Strengths shown (grading.md §4), labelled as guesses.
- The two or three places to fix first, then cards for every place the user got stuck.
- Then offer to practise the weakest answer in train mode.

## 4. Live mock with a real person ("just record it")

When a friend or mentor plays the interviewer and the user says "record only" or "this is the real run":
- Stop all coaching. Append what comes in, **as transcribed**, to `practice/YYYY-MM-DD-live-mock.md` and reply with
  one line ("Recorded.").
- When the transcript mixes two speakers, separate questions from answers.
- Do not record stories the other person tells about third parties (their company, their own interviews), only the
  advice and the questions.
- "Done" > the debrief of §3, plus the other person's advice turned into rules: each piece of advice becomes a line in
  `directions.md` (this interview) or `coaching.md` (general), with the user's confirmation.
- "Back to practice" > resume the normal loop.
